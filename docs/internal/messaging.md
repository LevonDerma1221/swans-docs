# Messaging and journals

## Transport
- **Aeron** (UDP unicast/multicast; IPC on one host) for the order path and market data.
- **gRPC** for request/reply: reference data queries, APIs.
- **Encoding:** Simple Binary Encoding (SBE); schemas in `schemas/`, C++ generated by `sbe-tool`. Every message: `{schema_id, version, seq, ts}`.

## Streams
`orders.in`, `orders.out`, `trades`, `book`, `refdata`, `settlement`, `collateral.balances`, `collateral.transfers`.

- `collateral.balances` — balance updates per account (available, locked) pushed by the collateral service, consumed by the engine pre-trade stage for balance checks.
- `collateral.transfers` — settlement, deposit, withdrawal, fee payment events, consumed by reconciliation and reporting.

Future (when margin offered): `risk.budget`, `pb.limits`, `pb.responses`.

## Journals
Every service has an append-only, checksummed journal of its inputs. State is a pure function of the journal — any service can recover by replaying from the last snapshot.

## Retention
Orders, trades, FIX messages: 7 years **[confirm RTS 24/25]**.
